Mocubix · Exhibit 06 · A running model, taken apart

The solar system, exploded

A working orrery. It opens on today, with every planet where it actually is, and runs at their true pace: one Earth year every four seconds, so Mercury laps while Neptune barely moves. Drag the slider and it comes apart into its layers without stopping.
