Mocubix · Exhibit 02 · From the space station, 2017–2024

Night side

Eight night passes of the International Space Station, laid end to end in the one direction it flies: east. Scroll down and the window moves sideways, and the station crosses the map with it. The distance you cover with your thumb becomes distance over the ground.

Pass 8 of 8 · Melbourne, 9 October 2024

22,858 km

Of night, from New York to Melbourne, always east: 49 minutes 44 seconds at the station’s speed.

A list stacks.
A window passes.

Down the page these would be eight separate pictures. Laid across it, in the order the station flies, they become one crossing of the night. They were taken years apart, by five crews; the route is the one they share. Always east, climbing toward 51.6° north and falling toward 51.6° south, as every orbit does.
