Mocubix · Exhibit 06 · A running model, taken apart

The solar system, exploded

A working orrery. Drag the slider and it comes apart into its layers — the bodies, the orbits, the light, the names — each lifted away from the others while every planet keeps moving. Nothing pauses to be explained.
