Mocubix · Exhibit 04 · Karl Blossfeldt, Urformen der Kunst, 1928

Every plant
on its own clock

The thin line at the very top is the page's progress: one number for the whole document. Beside each plate below is that plate's own progress, zero as it enters the screen and a hundred as it leaves. They disagree, and that disagreement is the whole piece.Scroll · watch the rail and the plates disagree

Pacing becomes editorial

When one scroll value drives every animation, everything arrives on the same beat and the reader stops noticing arrival. Give each element its own passage and rhythm becomes something you write: a dense plate can hold longer, a turn can land faster, and nothing on this page waits for anything else.
