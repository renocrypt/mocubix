Mocubix · exhibition slice 01

Seven ways
a page can move

Each exhibit is a real subject built on one mechanism — a mare, a storm, a volcano — and the mechanism is there to serve it. Open one and scroll.

House curve
cubic-bezier(0.16, 0.84, 0.24, 1) Fast departure, long quiet arrival. Every motion on these pages settles on it — the cards, the palette switch in the corner, the exhibits themselves.
01 · Muybridge, 1887

Annie G.

Sixteen frames of a galloping mare. Scroll and she runs; scroll back and she runs backwards. Your place on the scrollbar is the frame.

Open 02 · From the station window

Night side

Nine night passes over Earth, stitched into one window. Scroll down and the window moves sideways.

Open 03 · Hurricane Florence, 2018

Throw the page

A storm that listens to how fast you scroll, not where you are. Scroll gently and it turns; throw the page and it spins up.

Open 04 · Blossfeldt, 1928

Urformen

Ten plants, each growing into view on its own clock as it passes. The page has no single beat.

Open 05 · Secaucus Junction

Departures

A split-flap board that lives on its own clock. Trains board, depart and vanish whether you move or not.

Open 06 · A running model

Orrery

The solar system, pulled apart into its layers — bodies, orbits, light, names — while every planet keeps moving.

Open 07 · Hawaiian Volcano Observatory, 2018

Kīlauea, in four phases

An eruption in four phases with a live observatory panel. At each break the whole screen changes state at once — palette, photograph, alert, instruments — and nothing fades.
