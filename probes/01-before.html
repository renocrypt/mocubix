The bet that started cinema

In 1878 Leland Stanford wanted to know whether a galloping horse ever has all four hooves off the ground. Muybridge lined a track with twelve cameras tripped by wires and proved it: frames two and three. Nine years later, at Philadelphia, he shot Annie G. with the sequence above.

Reading a plate like this is exactly the gesture this page hangs on: you move, the animal moves; you stop, it stops; you go back, it goes back.
