Departures

Nothing on this page is driven by scroll. The board below runs on its own clock: flights board, go to final call, depart and vanish on seeded timers, and new ones drop in at the bottom. Stop moving entirely and it keeps living.

The last split-flap boards

Solari di Udine built these for stations and airports from the 1950s. Each character is a stack of printed flaps on a drum; a motor turns the drum until the right one falls forward, and a hall full of them sounds like rain. Most have been replaced by screens. Secaucus Junction still runs six.
