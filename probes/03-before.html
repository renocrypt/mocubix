Throw the page

Position says where you are. How fast you move is a signal of its own, and this storm listens to it. Scroll gently and it turns. Throw the page and it spins up.

Position is a where. Velocity is a how.

Every other page here answers to where you are. This one answers to how you are moving: it reacts at once and settles about six times more slowly, so a trackpad's small jitters never register and a hard throw reads as momentum. The same season, seen from orbit, below.
