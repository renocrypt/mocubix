The lake glows

Halemaʻumaʻu, spring 2018. A lake of lava has sat in the summit crater for ten years, lighting its own gas plume from below. The instruments read quiet.

USGS Hawaiian Volcano Observatory · Public domain

The ground opens

3 May. The rift zone cracks open along a residential street in Leilani Estates. Lava spatters through the palms. Everything on the panel just changed at once.

USGS Hawaiian Volcano Observatory · Public domain

03

Fissure 8 takes over

Twenty-four fissures have opened. One of them wins: a fountain eighty metres tall builds its own cone and feeds a river of lava that runs for weeks.

USGS Hawaiian Volcano Observatory · Public domain

▲ break · phase 04
04

Lava meets the sea

Kapoho. The river reaches the Pacific and boils it into a plume of acid steam. The bay is gone by July. Scroll back up and every break snaps in reverse, at the same line.

USGS Hawaiian Volcano Observatory · Public domain
